How it works

From first call to live automation in days

No 12-week consulting engagements. No bloated retainers. We move fast and show you something real early.

  1. Discovery

    Map your workflows

    A 45-minute call to understand your current processes, where the bottlenecks are, and which automation has the highest impact first.

  2. Build

    We build it

    Our team designs and deploys your automation. The first workflow is typically live within 72 hours of the kickoff call.

  3. Test

    Run in parallel

    New automation runs alongside your existing process. You can see exactly what it's doing before switching over fully. No risk.

  4. Scale

    Expand and optimise

    Once the first workflow is solid, we move to the next. Most clients automate 3–5 processes in the first 90 days.
